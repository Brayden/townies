## Allowed references

- Accurately describe your work as a contribution to, compatibility with, or an independent fork of Townies.
- Preserve required copyright and attribution notices, including links to https://github.com/Brayden/townies.
- Run unmodified branding privately for development, testing, or demonstration of a proposed contribution to the official project.

## Independent public projects

Use a distinct product name, logo, favicon, app icon, domain presentation, and marketing identity. Do not imply that your project is official, endorsed, or operated by Townies. A factual attribution such as “Based on the open-source Townies project” with a repository link is welcome.

A suggested visible credit is appreciated; Apache-2.0's actual attribution requirements are governed by LICENSE and NOTICE. This policy does not add an advertising or mandatory homepage-link clause to the software license.

The wordmark is rendered in `app/game/TownHeader.tsx` and account screens. Review `app/layout.tsx`, `public/manifest.webmanifest`, `public/favicon.svg`, authentication application names, and documentation when rebranding a public deployment.

For other uses of the branding, open an issue requesting written permission from @Brayden. Permission must not be assumed from silence, a submitted PR, or an accepted contribution.
