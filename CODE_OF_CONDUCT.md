# Community code of conduct

Townies should be welcoming to contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and maintainer decisions about project scope.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct directed at others, sharing private information without consent, and sustained disruptive behavior are not welcome. Do not use game or repository access to target other players or contributors.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and official project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or ban repeat or serious offenders. Enforcement should be proportionate and consider context; serious threats or privacy violations may justify immediate removal.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately using the repository's private reporting channel referenced in SECURITY.md. Include only the information needed for review. @Brayden handles reports and will avoid publishing identifying details unnecessarily. Do not retaliate against a person who reports a concern in good faith.
