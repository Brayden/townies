# Home interiors and neighborhood identity

Click your own front door to walk home and enter, press E beside it, or use Home → Go inside & decorate. Entry ends any active work shift and parks your vehicle. Outside returns to the same home's doorstep. Other residents' homes show their owner's name; they cannot be entered or edited in this installment.

The house opens as a Three.js dollhouse cutaway with a walkable room. Its default wall color, trim, curtains, and room title match the selected exterior design. Floor space grows from 8 × 7 at level 1 to 16 × 11 at level 5. Interiors match the exterior story count: levels 1–2 have one floor, levels 3–4 have two, and level 5 has three. Click the stairs, press E at a stair landing, or use the floor selector to switch stories. On a middle floor, the green/right stair arrow goes up and the blue/left arrow goes down. Each floor has separate furniture placement and wall/floor finishes. Click the floor, use movement keys, or use the mobile direction controls to walk around.

Choose Make it yours to decorate. Five starter pieces are already owned and placed. The collection has 22 pieces, including beds, sofas, chairs, tables, a desk, shelves, plants, lamps, and rugs. Choose a piece to preview it, tap the half-step floor grid or use the nudge controls, rotate it, and confirm placement. Paid pieces show the exact purchase cost before confirmation. Rearranging, storing, and placing owned pieces again are free. Each design can be owned once in this installment. Selecting an owned piece on another floor moves it to the current floor for free when placement is confirmed; its catalog label identifies its current floor.

Finishes offers seven wall choices (including matching the exterior) and five floors. These changes are free and save immediately. Furniture cannot overlap another solid piece or block the entry space. Rugs can sit under furniture. Invalid placements are explained and cannot be saved.

Room contents belong to the resident, so they travel with the resident to another address or town. When a smaller home cannot fit an existing piece, it remains owned and appears in Stored. No purchase is deleted. Upper-floor furniture and finishes are retained even if a downgrade removes that floor. Older room saves without floor numbers remain on the ground floor. Stairs and landings remain clear of furniture, including rugs. Larger homes can fit more furniture again.

Desktop hover gives each house a soft outline and a compact owner/address label. On touch screens, tapping the house shows that same label briefly. Vacancies say Available home. Labels use the saved property list, so owners do not need to be online. Names are inserted as text, never HTML. Clicking the owner's front-door target still enters the house directly.

Interior data, ownership, and purchase costs are server-owned. Entry validates the resident's actual address and proximity. Room updates validate membership, interior revision, current house dimensions, layout, and available coins before one atomic save. Duplicate purchases cannot debit twice. The player's outdoor position stays at their doorstep while indoors, and they are removed from outdoor peer rendering until they leave. Other residents do not receive their furniture inventory in shared property state.

Verification: `tests/interiors.mjs` checks all 50 doors and house designs, furniture footprints, entry authorization, purchases and retries, storage, finishes, moves, and downsizing. `tests/interiors-api.mjs` verifies the same room lifecycle and indoor/outdoor presence through the real local Worker with two test residents. Browser/device playtesting is separate; it was not requested for this implementation.

## Direct furniture placement

In Make it yours, choosing a catalog piece attaches a translucent preview to the desktop cursor. The half-step grid covers the actual room floor, and a colored footprint shows the occupied area: green fits, red is blocked. Clicking a valid spot buys and places it; the price is displayed before purchase. Owned pieces move for free. A simple click on existing furniture selects it; dragging keeps the grabbed offset and applies on release. Touch users can tap a position or slide the preview and release, with rotate/nudge/place buttons still available. R rotates and Escape cancels on desktop.

Invalid placements stay in preview and never spend coins. Releasing outside the room canvas or canceling a drag restores the saved arrangement. Saves retain the existing server collision, doorway/stairwell, coin, ownership and revision checks. Input is locked while saving or offline, and simultaneous pointers cannot take over an active drag.

## Neighbors, friends and visits

The Neighbors button is available outdoors and in homes. Its Online neighbors list includes people indoors; Add friend sends a mutual request. The recipient accepts or declines, and either player can cancel/remove the relationship. Accepted friends remain in the friends list across towns and can exchange private messages, including while one is offline. DMs are server-authorized to accepted friends, show unread counts and keep the most recent 60 messages visible. Sending is throttled and message IDs prevent duplicate retries. Removing a friend disables further messaging and access to the conversation until friends again.

Homes start closed. Under Neighbors → My home, choose Friends, Everyone (current town), or Nobody. Invitations last 30 minutes and respect the same access setting; they never bypass it. Online eligible neighbors can be invited over. Accept & visit takes the guest inside, and an eligible neighbor can also use Visit home or click the front door. Everyone allows visiting while the owner is offline. Visits do not cross town boundaries.

Guests see the owner's current decor, house style and floors, but only the owner can decorate or buy/store furniture. Indoor positions and floors are shared through the normal heartbeat; everyone on the same floor sees each other's characters, name labels, and wave/cheer/dance gestures. The room caption identifies neighbors on other floors. Leaving a visit returns the guest to the host's front door. Changing access, unfriending a friends-only guest, or moving the host to another town revokes the visit; revoked guests return outdoors. Friends and messaging are stored separately from town membership.
